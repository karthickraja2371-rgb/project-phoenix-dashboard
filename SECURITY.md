# 🛡️ SECURITY POLICY

## 1. Safety & Medical Device Disclaimer

**Project Phoenix** is an open-source assistive technology development project (Indian Provisional Patent Application No. 202641077314).

* **Zero Cloud Privacy Architecture**: The Syntiant NDP120 neural processor performs 100% offline edge inference. No sEMG muscle data, camera video feeds, or voice recordings are saved or transmitted to third-party cloud servers.
* **Hardware Interlocks**: System firmware enforces an analog hardware interrupt when socket skin pressure exceeds **20.0 kPa** to protect skin graft tissue.

---

## 2. Reporting a Vulnerability or Bug

If you discover a security flaw, firmware memory vulnerability, or safety interlock issue in **Project Phoenix**:

1. **Do NOT open a public issue** on GitHub.
2. Email the lead inventor directly at: `karthickraja2371@gmail.com` with the details of the issue.
3. Include step-by-step reproduction instructions and your environment specs (OS, MCU model, firmware version).

We will respond within 48 hours to acknowledge your report and coordinate a security patch before public disclosure.
