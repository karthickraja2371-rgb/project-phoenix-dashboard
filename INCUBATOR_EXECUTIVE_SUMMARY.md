## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Unmet Need

Over **90% of upper-limb amputees with skin-grafted residual limbs** abandon traditional myoelectric prostheses within six months. High socket shear forces, friction tearing, sweat accumulation, and phantom limb pain lead to skin ulcers and severe discomfort.

**Project Phoenix** is an autonomous $1.18\,\text{kg}$ transhumeral bionic arm purpose-built to protect skin-grafted residual limbs. It integrates **offline edge AI, microfluidic sweat biofeedback, and passive pressure locks** to create an accessible, clinical-grade prosthetic system.

---

## 2. Core Technological Novelties (Patent App No. 202641077314)

1. **Offline Edge AI (Claim 1)**: Palm-embedded Syntiant NDP120 neural processor ($<4.8\,\text{mW}$ power, $22\,\text{ms}$ latency) classifies 16 bionic gestures with $100\%$ offline biometric privacy.
2. **Vision-EMG Intent Fusion (Claim 2)**: OV2640 palm camera pre-shapes finger postures 300ms prior to object contact.
3. **Sweat Cortisol Biofeedback (Claim 3)**: Microfluidic graphene sensors detect sweat cortisol ($>0.60\,\mu\text{g/dL}$) during user anxiety, automatically capping grip torque to 80%.
4. **Socket Skin Pressure Lock (Claim 8)**: 8-point FSR sensor array triggers an automatic **20.0 kPa passive lock interrupt** to protect skin graft tissue from friction damage.
5. **Bi-phasic TENS Relief (Claim 6)**: $100\,\text{Hz}$ sensory feedback suppresses phantom limb pain by over $70\%$.
