🚀 Building Project Phoenix — Looking for Collaborators

After losing my right arm above the elbow and undergoing extensive skin grafting, I discovered that many existing prosthetic systems are not designed for people with skin-grafted residual limbs.

As a B.E. Electrical & Electronics Engineer, I decided to work on a solution.

Over the past several months, I've been developing Project Phoenix — an autonomous, offline AI prosthetic system concept focused on improving usability and safety for transhumeral amputees with skin-grafted residual limbs.

So far, I've completed:
✅ Indian Provisional Patent Application No. 202641077314 (Filed 23 June 2026)
✅ System Architecture & Master Engineering Documentation
✅ 3D CAD Models (Tripo3D Studio Model #42691fd0)
✅ WebGL Digital Twin & Interactive Dashboard
✅ Embedded Firmware C Architecture (STM32H753)
✅ KiCAD 8.0 Hardware Schematics (Syntiant NDP120 + TI ADS1299)
✅ Preliminary IEC 60601-1 & ISO 14971 Safety Audit
✅ GitHub Repository & Open-Source Governance Setup

I'm now looking to collaborate with passionate engineers, researchers, and mentors who are interested in:
🔹 Embedded Systems (STM32 / RTOS / CAN-FD)
🔹 PCB & Electronics Design (KiCAD 8.0)
🔹 Mechanical Design / 3D CAD (Titanium SLS / Dyneema Tendons)
🔹 Biomedical Engineering & sEMG Signal Processing
🔹 AI / Edge AI (Syntiant NDP120 Neural Inference)
🔹 Rehabilitation Engineering & Prosthetics Research

I'm not backed by a large company or research lab — I'm building this project from personal experience and with limited resources. My goal is to move from engineering design toward a physical prototype through collaboration, mentorship, and grant support.

If you're interested in contributing, mentoring, or simply sharing feedback, I'd love to connect!
